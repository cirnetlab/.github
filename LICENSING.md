# CIRNETLAB organization licensing policy

Effective 2026-11-25, by owner instruction, CIRNETLAB adopts the proprietary-first policy in [LICENSE](LICENSE) for eligible first-party materials. The GitHub organization is not itself proof of separate legal personality; the licensor is the lawful copyright holder(s). The prior organization policy is retained in Git history.

## Scope and exceptions

- New original first-party materials are proprietary by default, except expressly licensed components, valid existing grants, or mandatory law. Material in the pre-transition revision 8b8dd266b1f90df9c929aacb58bb1532e6ff2b2b retains any applicable prior license rights; this change does not revoke them. Existing material may be offered on separate proprietary terms only to the extent the rightsholder has authority; previously granted rights remain available.
- Preserve GPL, AGPL, Apache, MIT, BSD, OFL and other upstream terms where applicable. Multiple texts are not evidence of dual licensing. Map licenses to files, dependencies and distributable artifacts; unresolved scope or incompatible copyleft blocks proprietary distribution of the affected combination.
- CERN-OHL-W-2.0 and CC-BY-4.0 are options only for expressly approved hardware designs and educational/documentation assets respectively, not automatic grants. CC-BY allows commercial reuse.
- Preserve license notices, attribution, source and patent obligations. Do not assert rights in third-party material. Verify contributor rights and asset provenance before release.
- Repository LICENSING.md files document scope and component-specific exceptions; existing package metadata and historical license files must be reconciled when any release's effective terms change.
- CIRNETLAB and CIRNET branding require separate trademark review. No registered mark status or legal entity status is asserted.
- Keep confidential development repositories private when authorized. Public GitHub content remains subject to GitHub viewing/forking terms. Do not retroactively restrict lawful copies.
- Security policy: [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
