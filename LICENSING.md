# CIRNETLAB — Licensing and Intellectual Property Policy

Status: organization licensing direction and review procedure; not an operative relicensing of any existing material.
Proposed proprietary identifier: `LicenseRef-CIRNETLAB-Proprietary-1.0` (custom, not OSI-approved).

## Policy

CIRNETLAB adopts a proprietary-first direction for future software, documentation, data and hardware designs that its verified rightsholders own and are entitled to license. The intended default reserves copying, modification, redistribution, sublicensing, disclosure and commercial exploitation of expressly designated proprietary materials except as authorized in writing or permitted by applicable law. No new license grant or restriction on an existing grant is made by this policy.

Before adopting a proprietary license, record the legal rightsholder, ownership and contributor evidence, covered paths, exceptions, effective revision/date, approved contact and applicable distribution terms. Repository-level LICENSE and component-specific terms remain controlling for their respective scope. Earlier valid grants are unaffected. Do not remove copyright, license or attribution notices, or claim rights in third-party materials.

## Approved approach to third-party licensing

- Apache-2.0, MIT and BSD: preserve applicable license texts, attribution, patent terms and NOTICE where required.
- GPL-3.0 and AGPL-3.0: assess the actual covered work, source provision and distribution obligations; AGPL section 13 additionally applies to modified covered programs interacting remotely as specified by that license. No additional restriction on open-source rights. Unresolved compatibility blocks proprietary distribution of the affected combination.
- CERN-OHL-W-2.0: only for specifically designated or upstream covered hardware design materials; review source and conveyance obligations.
- CC-BY-4.0: only for expressly designated documentation, data or educational assets; attribution required and commercial reuse permitted.
- OFL-1.1: preserve font license and applicable naming obligations.
- Do not infer dual licensing from multiple license texts. Map each applicable license to the actual component. Do not relicense upstream code.

## Repository and release review

Each repository should record licensing scope in LICENSING.md and maintain accurate LICENSE, component license texts and third-party notices. Verify original and historical grants, contributors, dependency versions, generated and bundled artifacts, and applicable notices before a licensing transition. No placeholder is an effective proprietary license. Use repository-specific exceptions and exact SPDX identifiers when supported. Do not change functional source code as part of this licensing review.

## Brand and confidentiality

CIRNET and CIRNETLAB names, logos and mascots require separate ownership and trademark review. No trademark or endorsement permission is implied by a software license except as expressly provided or permitted by law. Do not claim registered status without evidence. Maintain appropriate access controls for confidential materials; public GitHub repositories permit viewing and forking under GitHub terms, and changing visibility does not revoke existing copies or grants. Separate authorization is required for visibility changes, registration, commercial terms and contributor agreements.
